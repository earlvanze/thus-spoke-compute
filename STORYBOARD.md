# Storyboard — THUS SPOKE COMPUTE ("AI is a normal technology?")

**Central metaphor: paper against the furnace.** The "normal technology" argument lives on **paper**: a working paper,
referee reports, slow S-curves, Gantt charts, footnotes, ink on bone, no glow. **Compute** is the **furnace**: near-black,
molten-orange type that blooms, and one acid-green **hyperbolic curve** (the only thing that owns `acid`). The song is
the paper being overrun. Paper owns the prologue, the economists and the bridge's math problems. The furnace takes every
chorus and keeps more of the frame each time. The outro goes back to paper for one word and then burns to the title.

Every composition is drawn from scratch in code (rules, arcs, type). Words are the objects: bricks, floors, ties,
rows on a conveyor belt, riders on a rope. All figures (cat, tractor, robots, boats, hammer) are original line art.
